# MAINTAINERS

Current maintainers of Surf FED. Maintainers have commit/merge rights and
triage responsibility. See `GOVERNANCE.md` for how the role works and how to
become one.

| Name / handle | Area of focus | Contact |
|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Surf FED Team | overall project, build/CI, main process | `maintainers@fedpromptly.com` |

## Responsibilities

- Triage new issues and discussions within a reasonable window.
- Review pull requests against `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the architecture rules
  in `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md` (in particular: keep `<webview>` +
  `session.loadExtension()` intact, keep `WEBVIEW_PARTITION` consistent).
- Ensure CI (`Build Desktop Packages`) is green on `main`.
- Cut releases by pushing a `v*` tag (see `DEPLOYMENT.md`).
- Handle private security reports per `SECURITY.md`.
